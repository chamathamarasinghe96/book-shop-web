Your Literary Home in Colombo

Since 2005, C BookHouse on Galle Road has been a beloved haven for readers in Sri Lanka. We're passionate about curating a world of books, fostering literary discovery, and creating a welcoming atmosphere where stories and ideas flourish.

Step inside and explore diverse collections, from global bestsellers and Sri Lankan narratives to academic texts and children's wonders. Our knowledgeable team is always ready with a recommendation. More than just a store, C BookHouse is a community hub where Colombo reads, connects, and gets inspired.
